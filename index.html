About text messages sent through SQuote

If you received a text message with a link to view a price quote, it was sent to you by the business named in that message (for example, "Joe's Plumbing") — not by SQuote. SQuote (a service operated by Nalvks LLC) is the software platform independent trade businesses (plumbers, electricians, HVAC techs, handymen, and similar) use to prepare and deliver quotes to their own customers.

Why you received a text

The business that texted you obtained your phone number directly from you when you contacted them about a job or requested a quote. At their direction, SQuote sent you one text message on their behalf. Example of the message you'd receive:

"Hi Jane, you have a new quote from Joe's Plumbing for $450.00. View it here: https://squote.app/joes-plumbing/quote/abc123"

This is a one-time transactional message tied to the quote you requested — not a recurring or marketing message. SQuote does not use your phone number for its own marketing, and does not share it with anyone outside the business that quoted you.

Consent

The business that texted you is responsible for having your consent to do so — given directly, when you provided your number to them for this purpose. SQuote acts only as the delivery platform they used to send it.

Message frequency & rates

Message frequency depends on how many quotes you request from businesses using SQuote. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out

Reply STOP to any message sent through SQuote to stop receiving further messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

Questions

For questions about a specific quote, contact the business named in the message you received. For questions about the SQuote platform itself: fastquoteapp@gmail.com
