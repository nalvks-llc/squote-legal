1. About the text messages you may receive

If you received a text message with a link to view a price quote, it was sent to you by the business named in that message (for example, "Joe's Plumbing") — not by SQuote. SQuote (a service operated by Nalvks LLC) is the software platform independent trade businesses (plumbers, electricians, HVAC techs, handymen, and similar) use to prepare and deliver quotes to their own customers.

Why you received a text

The business that texted you obtained your phone number directly from you when you contacted them about a job or requested a quote. At their direction, SQuote sent you one text message on their behalf. Example of the message you'd receive:

"Hi Jane, you have a new quote from Joe's Plumbing for $450.00. View it here: https://squote.app/joes-plumbing/quote/abc123. Reply STOP to opt out."

This is a one-time transactional message tied to the quote you requested — not a recurring or marketing message. SQuote does not use your phone number for its own marketing, and does not sell or share it with anyone outside the business that quoted you.

How your consent is obtained

Consent is given verbally, in the same conversation where the business quotes you the job — there is no separate web form. A typical exchange looks like this:

Business: "...So that comes out to $450 for the job. Want me to text you the quote so you've got it in writing?"
Customer: "Yes, that works."
Business: "Great — what's the best number to text it to?"
Customer: "[gives their phone number]"

The business then enters that number into SQuote when preparing your quote, and SQuote sends the single text message shown above. The business is responsible for obtaining your consent this way — SQuote acts only as the delivery platform they used to send it.

How consent is recorded

The phone number entry itself, made by the business inside the app at the moment they prepare your quote, is timestamped and tied to that specific quote — this is the record of when and why a message was sent to a given number. SQuote does not maintain a separate written consent form, since the business's request for your number and the resulting text happen in the same conversation, moments apart.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP to any message sent through SQuote to stop receiving further messages — this is enforced automatically and applies platform-wide, so no business using SQuote can text that number again once you've opted out. Reply START to resume receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

Message frequency & rates

Message frequency depends on how many quotes you request from businesses using SQuote — typically one message per quote. Message and data rates may apply.

Questions about a specific quote

Contact the business named in the message you received. For questions about the SQuote platform itself: fastquoteapp@gmail.com

2. Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Nalvks LLC ("we," "us"), operator of SQuote, collects and how we use it. It applies to two kinds of people: App users (the tradespeople who sign up for and use SQuote to run their business) and customers (the App user's own customers, who receive quotes).

What we collect

From App users: name, business name and contact details, email, phone, password (handled entirely by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your raw password), and billing information (handled entirely by Stripe — we never see or store your card number).

About customers: only what's needed to deliver a quote — name, and an email address or phone number (at least one, to send it). We deliberately do not collect a customer's home address or other details as part of the quote flow, even though our database has optional fields reserved for a possible future customer-management feature.

How we use it

  • To operate the App user's account and the quotes they create
  • To deliver quotes to their customers by email and/or SMS, at the App user's direction
  • To process subscription billing
  • To improve the AI-assisted quote-drafting feature

We do not sell personal information — including phone numbers — to anyone, and we do not use customer contact information for our own marketing.

Service providers we use

We use a small number of providers to operate SQuote. Each processes data only as needed to perform its function for us, under its own security and privacy commitments:

ProviderPurpose
Firebase (Google)Account sign-in and authentication
SupabaseDatabase hosting
SendGrid (Twilio)Delivering quote and account emails
TwilioDelivering quote SMS messages
StripeSubscription billing and payment processing
AnthropicAI assistance for drafting quotes from voice/text input

Data retention

We retain account and quote data for as long as an App user's account is active, so they retain their own quote history. An App user may request deletion of their account and associated data by contacting us below; some records may be retained briefly afterward where required for legitimate business or legal purposes (e.g. billing records).

Security

SQuote is multi-tenant software: every App user's data is isolated at the database level so one business can never see another's quotes, customers, or account details. We use industry-standard practices (encrypted connections, access controls) to protect data in transit and at rest.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting fastquoteapp@gmail.com. If you're a customer who received a quote and want your information removed, you can make the same request, or ask the business that quoted you.

Children's privacy

SQuote is a business tool intended for adults operating a trade business, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SQuote changes. We'll update the "last updated" date below when we do.
